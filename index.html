Research

Human-centered questions,
real-world robot systems.

I connect information systems, HRI, and service research to understand how embodied AI can act socially, recover from failure, and coordinate in environments with multiple people and robots.

01

Multi-party HRI & collaboration

How can several robots recognize, prioritize, and adapt to several customers without losing social context?

02

Frontline service robot design

Designing embodied systems around situational context, service goals, and the realities of deployment.

03

Failure recovery & trust repair

Understanding which robot responses rebuild confidence after service failures—and when they work.

04

Explainability through social cues

Examining how embodied and social signals make robot decisions more understandable during collaboration.

05

VR & MR for learning

Comparing immersive environments and their structural affordances for spatial learning.

About

My research examines how AI artifacts, such as robots, are incorporated into organizational settings and how their design, use, and interaction with users shape perceptions, evaluations, and behavioral outcomes.

Research Profile

I focus on embodied and immersive technologies—including physical robots and virtual reality—as well as digital information environments such as online news platforms.

My work uses mixed methods, combining laboratory and online experiments with open-ended online interviews and semi-structured interviews. I also collaborate with industry partners, including Hyundai Motor Company Robotics Lab and Samsung Electronics, to investigate real-world challenges at the intersection of AI, robotics, and human–technology interaction.
